The Socratic Pause // Think before you answer

A question worth sitting with.

If a belief feels comforting, does that make it more likely to be true—or only more difficult to question?

Separate the emotional value of a belief from the evidence offered in its support. A claim deserves the same scrutiny whether it comforts you or unsettles you.
